About

Hi, I'm Katharina [kataˈʁiːna], a PhD student at the Trustworthy AI Lab at the University of Hamburg, supervised by Anne Lauscher and Jae Hee Lee. I research mechanistic interpretability, bias mitigation, multilinguality, and multi-agent systems, with a focus on ethical alignment. The goal is language models that are fairer and more trustworthy across languages and cultures. Send me a message if you are interested in collaborating. You can also talk to me about hiking, painting and cats.
